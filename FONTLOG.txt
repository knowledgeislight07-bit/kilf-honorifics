FONTLOG for KILF Honorifics and KILF Naskh
==========================================

This file records the history of KILF Honorifics and KILF Naskh, as the SIL Open Font License
recommends for fonts derived from other OFL fonts.

Basic font information
----------------------
KILF Honorifics is a small companion font holding the Arabic honorific ligatures of Unicode
(ﷺ ﷻ ﷾ ﷿ ﷲ and U+FD40–U+FD4F, such as ﵁ ﵂ ﵄ ﵀ ﵇). It is meant to sit beside EB Garamond in
English text, in print and in EPUB, for the publications of Knowledge Is Light Foundation (KILF).

The glyph designs are those of Noto Naskh Arabic. KILF Honorifics changes only their size,
position and spacing, and the font's line metrics.

ChangeLog
---------
1.001 (2026-11-18), Knowledge Is Light Foundation
- Fix: cap height and x-height (OS/2 sCapHeight, sxHeight) copied from EB Garamond (650 / 400).
  1.000 left them at 0, and Typst, which spaces lines from the cap height, then fell back to the
  ascender: every line holding an honorific stood about 0.36 em further from the line above.
  Glyphs and spacing are otherwise unchanged.

1.000 (2026-11-18), Knowledge Is Light Foundation
- First release. Built by build.py from Noto Naskh Arabic 2.021 (glyphs) and EB Garamond 1.003
  (line metrics and optical target).
- 21 honorific ligatures, decomposed from Noto's components and scaled to 85%: ﷺ runs from
  -0.15 em to +0.75 em, i.e. from just below the baseline to about EB Garamond's ascender.
- Optical centring: the raḍiya-llāhu ʿanhu family and its neighbours (U+FD40–U+FD4F) are raised
  75% of the way toward the ink centre of EB Garamond text (+0.21 em). Noto's versions carry
  most of their ink at or below the baseline and otherwise look sunk beside English.
- Ascender, descender and line gap copied from EB Garamond, so the font never makes a line
  taller; this matters in EPUB readers, where a fallback font's metrics can widen line spacing.
- 30-unit side bearings on each glyph.

KILF Naskh
----------
KILF Naskh is Noto Naskh Arabic with EB Garamond's line metrics, for Arabic words and phrases
inside English text. The glyph designs, joining, ligatures and mark positioning are Noto's,
unchanged.

1.001 (2026-11-18), Knowledge Is Light Foundation
- Fix: cap height and x-height copied from EB Garamond (650 / 400), as in KILF Honorifics 1.001.
  1.000 left them at 0, which made Typst add about 0.36 em above any line holding Arabic.

1.000 (2026-11-18), Knowledge Is Light Foundation
- First release. Built by build_naskh.py from Noto Naskh Arabic 2.021 and EB Garamond 1.003
  (line metrics only).
- Ascender, descender and line gap (hhea, OS/2 typo and win) copied from EB Garamond: 1007 /
  -298 / 0, in place of Noto's 1069 / -634 / 0. A line holding Arabic is no taller than one
  without it.
- Subset to Arabic, Arabic Supplement, Arabic Extended-A, both presentation-form blocks, and
  the spaces, joiners, direction marks and punctuation used in Arabic text (1201 characters).
  Arabic Extended-B and -C are left out. All OpenType features are kept; HarfBuzz shapes
  plain, vocalised and Quranic samples identically to Noto Naskh Arabic.
- Glyphs at Noto's size (SCALE 1.0). Renamed, with Noto's trademark notice removed and its
  copyright and designer credits kept.
