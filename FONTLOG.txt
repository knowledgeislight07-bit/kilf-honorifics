FONTLOG for KILF Honorifics
===========================

This file records the history of KILF Honorifics, as the SIL Open Font License recommends
for fonts derived from other OFL fonts.

Basic font information
----------------------
KILF Honorifics is a small companion font holding the Arabic honorific ligatures of Unicode
(ﷺ ﷻ ﷾ ﷿ ﷲ and U+FD40–U+FD4F, such as ﵁ ﵂ ﵄ ﵀ ﵇). It is meant to sit beside EB Garamond in
English text, in print and in EPUB, for the publications of Knowledge Is Light Foundation (KILF).

The glyph designs are those of Noto Naskh Arabic. KILF Honorifics changes only their size,
position and spacing, and the font's line metrics.

ChangeLog
---------
1.000 (2026-11-18), Knowledge Is Light Foundation
- First release. Built by build.py from Noto Naskh Arabic 2.021 (glyphs) and EB Garamond 1.003
  (line metrics and optical target).
- 21 honorific ligatures, decomposed from Noto's components and scaled to 85%: ﷺ runs from
  -0.15 em to +0.75 em, i.e. from just below the baseline to about EB Garamond's ascender.
- Optical centring: the raḍiya-llāhu ʿanhu family and its neighbours (U+FD40–U+FD4F) are raised
  75% of the way toward the ink centre of EB Garamond text (+0.21 em). Noto's versions carry
  most of their ink at or below the baseline and otherwise look sunk beside English.
- Ascender, descender and line gap copied from EB Garamond, so the font never makes a line
  taller; this matters in EPUB readers, where a fallback font's metrics can widen line spacing.
- 30-unit side bearings on each glyph.
